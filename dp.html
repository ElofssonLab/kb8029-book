Dynamic programming, interactively

A bench for the three modes of renderDP(). Change the settings, press Rebuild, and all three panels below redraw.

Settings
Presets:

1 · Step through it

Walk the fill one cell at a time. The blue cell is the one being decided; the three yellow cells are the only ones it is allowed to look at. Each of them carries a badge with the value it offers, and the winning offer is green — that is the max in the recurrence, happening in front of you.

2 · Guess the next value

Everything above and to the left is already filled in. Work out the blue cell and type it.

3 · Fill the whole matrix

Type every value yourself. Check marks each cell; the traceback appears once the grid is right.
